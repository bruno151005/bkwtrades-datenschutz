4. Purpose and legal basis (GDPR)

  • Delivering the requested material (indicator, study PDFs, server invite) — Art. 6(1)(b): you explicitly asked for it by commenting the keyword.
  • Replying to your messages — Art. 6(1)(b).
  • Understanding which posts prompt responses — Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest in improving my own content. This analysis is aggregated and is not used to evaluate you as an individual.

5. Recipients

Data is stored on a server operated by me and is never sold, rented or passed on to advertisers. Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. is technically involved as the operator of Instagram, since both the comment and the message pass through their platform in any case. The Instagram Privacy Policy applies in addition.

6. Retention

Contact records are deleted after 24 months without any interaction — or earlier, as soon as you ask for deletion.

7. Your rights

  • Access to the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16) and erasure (Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21)
How to request deletion: An informal email to bkwbusiness@gmail.com, or a direct message to @bkwtrades containing the word delete, is enough. Your data will then be removed in full, normally within 30 days. No reason is required.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority here is the Thuringian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (TLfDI), Häßlerstraße 8, 99096 Erfurt, Germany.

8. Automated decision-making

The reply is triggered automatically once a keyword is detected. No profiling and no automated decision producing legal effects within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

9. Changes

If the processing changes, this page will be updated. The version published here, with the date shown above, is the one that applies.
